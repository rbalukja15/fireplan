        What this model does not know

        True of every result on this page, whatever the banner above says. These are gaps in the measurement record, not in the arithmetic.

        • Land unit vs. a different land unit. Attack is known to depend on the target's class, and only same-class pairings were ever measured. 90 of the 100 land pairings are extrapolation.
        • Terrain. This page models a direct attack. Patrol was measured separately and behaves differently — same base stat, far less of the attacker's own attrition charged against it — and is not modelled here. Debark is unknown.
        • Multi-round battles. Every measurement used exactly one round.
        • Variance. The game rolls roughly ±10%. Nothing here is rolled; these are the variance-off figures.
        • Heroes, positioning and range. Never exercised. All measurements sit at position 0 with no hero.
        • The Balloon. Not one quantity was ever measured — the calculator refuses to accept it in the air.
