    Every measurement behind this model used exactly one round.

    Embarkation is a class change: a non-naval unit in sea or debark attacks and defends on a flat column (1.0, or 0.5 against air), holds a flat 10 HP whatever it is, and is hit on the attacker’s naval column.

    Terrain is per side, and the target’s is what picks the coefficient column: a battleship deals 6.0 per effective unit to fighters in the air and 40.0 to the same fighters embarked at sea.

    Range is a yes/no gate, not a falloff, and every unit's is measured by bisection: melee 5 km, light artillery 30, artillery 50, cruiser 40, battleship 75, railgun 150. A row that cannot reach is inert. Past 5 km the defender does not fire back at all.
