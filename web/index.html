        What this model does not know

        True of every result on this page, whatever the banner above says. These are gaps in the measurement record, not in the arithmetic.

        • Land unit vs. a different land unit. Attack is known to depend on the target's class, and only same-class pairings were ever measured. 90 of the 100 land pairings are extrapolation.
        • Terrain. Air stacks can be flown as a direct strike or on patrol, and both are modelled — but patrol's attrition coefficient is a band (0.360–0.427 over nine cells), not a number, so every patrol result is an estimate. Debark has never been submitted, and no other terrain modifier was measured.
        • Mixed stacks beyond two types. That a stack saturates as a whole in roster order, and that incoming damage splits by attack × count, are both measured exactly — but on two-type land mixtures only (infantry with artillery, four layouts). Three types, or a mixture spanning classes, was never submitted.
        • Multi-round battles. Every measurement used exactly one round.
        • Positioning and range. Never exercised. Every measurement sits at position 0, so artillery (50 km) and railguns (150 km) were read standing next to the enemy.
        • Variance. The game rolls roughly ±10%. Nothing here is rolled; these are the variance-off figures.
        • Heroes. Fully modelled on land, in both channels: a hero fights as one unit with its own HP pool, takes a share of every round, raises the output of specific unit types, and separately raises their max HP. A hero has two attack columns like a unit does — Pershing attacks at 62 and defends at 8 — and some buffs are defence-only (Joffre’s and Kangal’s measure exactly zero on an attacking stack). Every curve is stored as measured points and interpolated, never fitted: Pershing’s infantry HP buff climbs to ×1.70 by level 5, drops to ×1.10 at level 6, and climbs again. What is still missing: the six heroes the server refuses on land (Otto, Tōgō, Ivan, Richthofen, von Thaden) do work on air and naval stacks and all six change the battle — measured, but not yet split into own-attack and multiplier, so no hero effect is applied there.
        • The Balloon. Not one quantity was ever measured — the calculator refuses to accept it in the air.
